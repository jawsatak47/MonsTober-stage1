The Battle Format

Five selected artists take the stage at the same time for a live, timed illustrative showdown. Everyone works the same brief, the same materials, and the same clock — the only variable is what each artist brings to life.

The brief: bring a monster to the canvas. A favorite creature, or something born on the spot. No color to hide behind — just line, contrast, and nerve.

  • 5 artists, 1v1v1v1v1, live on stage
  • Subject: an original or favorite monster
  • Palette: strict black & white, two-tone
  • Medium: black Sharpie + white acrylic paint
  • Surface: 30"×20" or 18"×24" black canvas
  • All materials supplied to the artist
